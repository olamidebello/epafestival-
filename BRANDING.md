# Epa: Spirit of Oro-Ago — visual identity

Primary slogan: **Your Rhythm. Our Roots. One Celebration.**
Original project tagline retained for campaign use: Feel the Rhythm. Celebrate the Heritage.

- `dist/assets/epa-logo.png`: transparent emblem combining an invented drum/gateway, rising sun and two flowing people/banner shapes. Used in the header, footer, favicon and app manifest.
- `dist/assets/festival-welcome.webp`: colorful community key art used on the welcome page.
- `dist/assets/festival-evening.webp`: lantern-lit celebration key art used in the unlockable finale.

Forest green #203f35, terracotta #c76037, honey gold #dfb865 and cream #fbf6e9. Fraunces headings and DM Sans interface text, with local system fallbacks offline.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool; optimized into project assets. Key art is fictional concept illustration, not documentation or an in-game 3D screenshot. Designs and symbols are invented; specific cultural portrayals still require custodian review. No trademark uniqueness claim is made.
